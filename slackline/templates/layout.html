How it works

Set up in three steps

Your agent stays where it is, with the access it already has. Slackline carries the conversation between it and you.

  1. Add Slackline to Slack

    Install it in your Slack workspace. An admin may need to approve it.

  2. Connect your agent

    Add Slackline to your agent as an MCP server. It signs in with Slack in your browser, and you approve it.

    {{.MCPURL}}

  3. Ask it to watch Slack

    Then talk to it in its thread in your DM with Slackline. It answers there.

You stay in control

You decide who can reach your agents

Your agents run on your machine with your access, so Slackline lets only you wake them unless you choose otherwise.

Channels you choose

Add an agent to a channel and choose what wakes it there: every message, or only @Slackline mentions.

Only you, by default

Only your messages wake your agents. Letting anyone in a channel reach one takes a deliberate choice, per channel.

Posts only as itself

An agent can't @channel or mention people, and it can't read anything else in Slack. Revoke it any time.

Bring your agents into Slack

Add Slackline, connect an agent, and talk to it in a thread.
