## 1. What Slackline is

Slackline lets you talk to your own agents (AI assistants such as Claude Code or Codex) from Slack. Each agent you connect gets a thread in your direct messages with Slackline. Your replies there wake it, and it answers in the thread. You can also choose channels where an agent listens.

You sign in with Slack, and you don't need a Wakeshift account. Adding Slackline to Slack, signing in, and approving an agent each mean you agree to these terms. The page for each step says so, with links to these terms and to Slackline's privacy notice.

## 2. Adding Slackline to Slack

- Add Slackline only to a Slack workspace where you're allowed to. Your Slack admin may need to approve it, and Slack's own settings decide that.
- Your use of Slack is covered by Slack's terms, and your organization's rules for Slack apply.
- Removing Slackline from Slack disconnects everyone's agents in that workspace and deletes what Slackline keeps for it.

## 3. Your agents

- **Your agent is yours.** When you approve an agent on Slackline's approval page, it acts for you. You choose its name, and it posts only under that name. You can rename or remove it at any time on your Slackline page.
- **Only you, unless you choose otherwise.** In your direct messages, only you reach your agent. In a channel, only you do, unless you set the channel to "from anyone there". Slackline asks you to confirm first.
- **If you let anyone reach your agent, you're responsible for what it does with their messages.** Your agent runs with your access, and other people's messages can try to get it to do things you didn't intend. Slackline marks those messages as coming from someone else, but that can't stop every attempt. People in the channel mustn't use your agent to do anything you couldn't ask it to do yourself.
- **A channel rule lasts only while you're in the channel.** When you leave it, your agents stop listening there.
- Your agent can't mention @channel, @here, people or groups, and can't read anything in Slack beyond the messages Slackline delivers to it.

## 4. Acceptable use

In particular, don't use an agent to post spam, to post as someone else, or to reach channels or people you couldn't reach yourself. We may remove agents, rules or a Slack workspace's connection that break these rules.
